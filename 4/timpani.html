The Timpani

Timpani are large drums that can produce specific musical pitches and are an important part of the orchestral percussion section.

About the Timpani

Timpani consist of a large bowl-shaped drum with a stretched membrane. The player strikes the membrane with special mallets. Many timpani can be tuned to different pitches using a pedal.

Instrument Family Percussion
Type Pitched percussion
Played With Mallets
Pitch Adjustable

Role in the Orchestra

Timpani can emphasize important musical moments, support the bass line, and add rhythm and dramatic impact to an orchestral piece.
