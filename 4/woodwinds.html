The Woodwind Family

The woodwind family includes instruments that produce sound by directing air through the instrument. Some use reeds while others, such as the flute, use an opening to create the sound.

Choose an Instrument

Select one of the instruments below to learn more about its role and characteristics.

Flute

The flute produces sound by directing air across an opening. It is known for its clear and bright tone.

Learn About the Flute

Oboe

The oboe uses a double reed and has a distinctive, focused sound that can easily stand out in an orchestra.

Learn About the Oboe

Clarinet

The clarinet uses a single reed and has a wide range of tones, from warm low notes to bright high notes.

Learn About the Clarinet

Bassoon

The bassoon is a large double-reed instrument with a deep and distinctive sound.
