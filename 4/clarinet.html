The Clarinet

The clarinet is a single-reed woodwind instrument with a wide range and a smooth, flexible sound.

About the Clarinet

The clarinet produces sound using a single reed attached to its mouthpiece. Players use keys along the instrument to control its pitch. The clarinet can produce both low and high notes.

Instrument Family Woodwind
Typical Range Wide
Reed Single reed
Common Material Wood

Role in the Orchestra

Clarinet parts can include melodies, harmonies, and supporting passages. Its wide range allows it to fill several different roles within an orchestral arrangement.
