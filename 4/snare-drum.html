The Snare Drum

The snare drum is a percussion instrument known for its sharp, crisp sound and rhythmic versatility.

About the Snare Drum

A snare drum has a set of wires or metal strands stretched across the bottom head of the drum. When the drum is struck, these snares create its distinctive buzzing sound.

Instrument Family Percussion
Type Unpitched percussion
Played With Drumsticks
Sound Sharp and rhythmic

Role in the Orchestra

The snare drum can provide rhythmic patterns, accents, and dramatic effects. It is also commonly used in military and marching music.
