The Bassoon

The bassoon is a large double-reed woodwind instrument known for its deep and distinctive sound.

About the Bassoon

The bassoon uses a double reed connected to a long folded tube. Its large size allows it to produce low notes while still being capable of playing a wide range of pitches.

Instrument Family Woodwind
Typical Range Low to middle
Reed Double reed
Common Material Wood

Role in the Orchestra

Bassoons often provide bass lines and harmonic support for the woodwind section. They can also play melodies and are sometimes used for humorous or distinctive musical effects.
