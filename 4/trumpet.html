The Trumpet

The trumpet is a bright and powerful brass instrument commonly used for melodies and fanfare-like passages.

About the Trumpet

Trumpets produce sound when the player vibrates their lips against the mouthpiece. Valves are used to change the length of the tubing and produce different notes.

Instrument Family Brass
Typical Range High
Valves Usually 3
Common Material Brass

Role in the Orchestra

Trumpets often play strong melodies, rhythmic parts, and dramatic musical passages. Their bright sound allows them to stand out from many other instruments.
