The Double Bass

The double bass is the largest common orchestral string instrument. Its low register provides an important foundation for the rest of the string section and orchestra.

Instrument Information

Family Strings
Common Role Low foundation and rhythmic support
Typical Range E1–G4
How It Produces Sound Vibrating strings

How the Double Bass Is Used

The double bass often reinforces the lowest notes of the orchestra. It can work with cellos, bassoons, tubas, and other low instruments to create a strong foundation.

Like other orchestral strings, the double bass can be played with a bow or by plucking the strings. Its large size allows it to produce very low sounds.
