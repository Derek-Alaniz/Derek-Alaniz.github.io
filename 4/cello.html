The Cello

The cello is a large string instrument with a rich and expressive sound. It can perform melodies while also supporting the lower part of an ensemble.

Instrument Information

Family Strings
Common Role Melody, harmony, and bass support
Typical Range C2–C6
How It Produces Sound Vibrating strings

How the Cello Is Used

The cello can provide a strong foundation for an orchestral texture while also playing expressive melodies. Its range allows it to work with both middle and lower musical parts.

Cellists normally play while seated, with the instrument supported by an endpin. The strings can be played with a bow or plucked with the fingers.
