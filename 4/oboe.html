The Oboe

The oboe is a double-reed woodwind instrument recognized for its distinctive, focused tone.

About the Oboe

The oboe produces sound when air passes through two small pieces of reed placed together at the top of the instrument. Oboes are usually made from wood and have a system of keys used to produce different notes.

Instrument Family Woodwind
Typical Range High
Reed Double reed
Common Material Wood

Role in the Orchestra

The oboe frequently performs important melodic passages and can provide expressive sounds that help give orchestral music its character.
