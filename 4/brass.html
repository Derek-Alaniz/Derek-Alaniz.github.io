The Brass Family

Brass instruments produce sound when the player vibrates their lips against a mouthpiece. They can provide powerful melodies, harmonies, accents, and low musical foundations.

Choose an Instrument

Select one of the instruments below to learn more about its role and characteristics.

Trumpet

The trumpet has a bright sound and is commonly used for melodies, fanfares, and strong musical accents.

Learn About the Trumpet

French Horn

The French horn has a rounded sound and can connect the brass and woodwind sections with its flexible tone.

Learn About the French Horn

Trombone

The trombone uses a slide to change pitch and is capable of powerful low and middle-register sounds.

Learn About the Trombone

Tuba

The tuba is the lowest common orchestral brass instrument and provides a strong bass foundation.

Learn About the Tuba
