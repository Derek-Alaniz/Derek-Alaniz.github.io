The Tuba

The tuba is the largest common brass instrument and is known for producing deep, low-pitched sounds.

About the Tuba

The tuba produces sound when the player vibrates their lips into a mouthpiece. Valves are used to change the length of the tubing and control the instrument's pitch.

Instrument Family Brass
Typical Range Low
Valves Usually 3 to 6
Common Material Brass

Role in the Orchestra

Tubas usually provide the foundation of the brass section by playing low notes and supporting the harmony and rhythm of orchestral music.
