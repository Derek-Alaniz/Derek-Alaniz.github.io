The Flute

The flute is a woodwind instrument known for its bright and clear sound. It is one of the highest-pitched instruments commonly found in an orchestra.

About the Flute

Unlike many woodwind instruments, the flute does not use a reed. The player creates sound by blowing air across an opening near the mouthpiece. Flutes are commonly made from metal and use keys to control the instrument's notes.

Instrument Family Woodwind
Typical Range High
Reed None
Common Material Metal

Role in the Orchestra

Flutes often play melodies, countermelodies, and decorative passages. Their high register allows them to stand out above many of the other instruments in the orchestra.
