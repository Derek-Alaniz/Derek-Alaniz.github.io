The Bass Drum

The bass drum is a large percussion instrument that produces powerful, deep sounds.

About the Bass Drum

The bass drum is a large cylindrical drum that is usually struck with a large soft mallet. Its size allows it to produce very low and powerful sounds.

Instrument Family Percussion
Type Unpitched percussion
Played With Large mallet
Sound Deep and powerful

Role in the Orchestra

The bass drum is often used to emphasize important moments and create dramatic impact. It can also support the rhythm and intensity of a musical passage.
