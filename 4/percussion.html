The Percussion Family

The percussion family includes instruments that are struck, shaken, scraped, or otherwise activated to create sound. Percussion can provide rhythm, accents, atmosphere, and dramatic effects.

Timpani

Timpani are tuned drums that can provide both rhythmic emphasis and definite pitches.

Snare Drum

The snare drum has a crisp sound created by wires stretched across the bottom drumhead.

Bass Drum

The bass drum produces a deep, resonant sound and is often used for large accents and dramatic moments.

Learn About the Bass Drum

Cymbals

Cymbals are metal percussion instruments used for accents, crashes, and changes in musical intensity.
