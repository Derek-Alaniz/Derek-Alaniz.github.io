The Violin

The violin is the highest-pitched standard member of the string family. It is one of the most recognizable instruments in an orchestra.

Instrument Information

Family Strings
Common Role Melody and harmony
Typical Range G3–E7

How the Violin Is Used

Violins are frequently used to play melodies because of their high register and ability to move quickly between notes. They can also play chords, harmony, and repeated rhythmic patterns.

In a large orchestra, violins are usually divided into first and second violin sections. These sections can play different musical parts at the same time.
