The Trombone

The trombone is a brass instrument recognized by its long slide and powerful sound.

About the Trombone

Unlike most brass instruments, the trombone commonly uses a slide instead of valves to change pitch. Moving the slide changes the length of the tubing through which the air travels.

Instrument Family Brass
Typical Range Middle to low
Pitch Mechanism Slide
Common Material Brass

Role in the Orchestra

Trombones provide powerful harmonies, bass lines, and dramatic musical passages. They are especially useful when an orchestra needs a strong and bold brass sound.
