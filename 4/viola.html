The Viola

The viola is slightly larger than the violin and has a lower, darker sound. It plays an important role in the middle range of the string section.

Instrument Information

Family Strings
Common Role Harmony and inner melodies
Typical Range C3–A6
How It Produces Sound Vibrating strings

How the Viola Is Used

The viola often fills the middle register between the violins and lower string instruments. Its darker tone can help connect different sections of the orchestra.

Violists may play harmony, supporting rhythms, or melodies. Although the instrument is similar in appearance to the violin, its larger size gives it a deeper sound.
