About Orchestra Explorer

Orchestra Explorer is an educational web application designed to introduce users to the instruments that make up a modern orchestra. The application organizes instruments into four major families: strings, woodwinds, brass, and percussion.

Each instrument family has its own collection of instruments, each with different physical characteristics, sounds, and roles within an ensemble. By organizing the information into smaller sections, Orchestra Explorer makes it easier to explore the orchestra one instrument at a time.

The Four Instrument Families

Strings

String instruments produce sound primarily through vibrating strings. The violin, viola, cello, and double bass form the core of the orchestral string family.

Woodwinds

Woodwind instruments produce sound using a combination of air, keys, and in some cases reeds. The flute, oboe, clarinet, and bassoon are common orchestral woodwinds.

Brass

Brass instruments create sound when players vibrate their lips against a mouthpiece. Trumpets, French horns, trombones, and tubas provide powerful sounds across a wide range of pitches.

Percussion

Percussion instruments create sound by being struck, shaken, or otherwise activated. Timpani, snare drums, bass drums, and cymbals can provide rhythm, emphasis, and dramatic effects.

How to Use the App

  1. Choose an instrument family from the home page.
  2. Choose an individual instrument.
  3. Read about the instrument and its role in the orchestra.
  4. Return to the family selection to explore another instrument.
