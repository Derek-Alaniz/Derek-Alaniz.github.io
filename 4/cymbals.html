The Cymbals

Cymbals are percussion instruments made from metal that can create bright crashes and sustained sounds.

About the Cymbals

Orchestral cymbals are usually played by striking two cymbals together or by using a mallet. The resulting sound can range from a short accent to a long, powerful crash.

Instrument Family Percussion
Type Unpitched percussion
Common Material Metal
Sound Bright and resonant

Role in the Orchestra

Cymbals are often used to emphasize important moments, transitions, and climaxes. Their sound can add excitement and intensity to an orchestral performance.
