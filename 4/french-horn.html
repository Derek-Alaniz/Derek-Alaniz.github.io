The French Horn

The French horn is a brass instrument known for its rounded shape and warm, mellow sound.

About the French Horn

The French horn has a long length of coiled tubing. Players use valves to change the length of the tubing and produce different pitches. The player's hand can also be placed inside the bell to affect the sound.

Instrument Family Brass
Typical Range Middle to high
Valves Usually 3 or 4
Common Material Brass

Role in the Orchestra

French horns are often used to create rich harmonies and support melodies. They can blend with both the woodwind and brass sections.
